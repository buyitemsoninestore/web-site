Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

How long does the delivery take? +

Our delivery system is highly automated. **95% of orders are delivered within 5-15 minutes.** In rare cases of high demand or technical maintenance, it may take up to 2-4 hours, but we guarantee delivery within 24 hours.

Are these accounts Private or Shared? +

We offer both options depending on the plan you choose. **Private accounts** allow you to use your own email and have full control, while **Shared slots** are a more budget-friendly way to enjoy premium features securely.

Do I get a full warranty? +

Yes, absolutely! Every purchase comes with a **Full Duration Warranty.** If your subscription stops working for any reason, we will provide a replacement or fix it within 24 hours. Your satisfaction is our priority.

Is it safe to use these in Sri Lanka? +

Yes, all our premium services are 100% safe and compatible with Sri Lankan networks. We use legitimate methods to provide access, ensuring your personal devices and data remain secure.

Can I renew my existing subscription? +

For most services like YouTube and Spotify, we can upgrade your **existing personal account.** For others like Netflix, we provide new profile credentials. Check the 'Plan Details' for specific information.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept a wide variety of payments: **Bank Transfers** (BOC, Sampath, HNB, etc.), **LankaQR** (KOKO, Frimi, Genie), and all **Credit/Debit Cards** via our encrypted secure gateway.

How can I get help if I have an issue? +

Our support team is available **24/7.** You can click the WhatsApp icon on the bottom right to chat with us directly, or use our AI Chatbot for instant answers to common technical questions.

Can I use the service on multiple devices? +

Yes, most services allow simultaneous streaming. For example, **Netflix** depends on your chosen plan (1-4 screens), while **YouTube Premium** can be used on all your signed-in devices.

Do I need a VPN to use these services? +

No VPN is required. All our subscriptions are globally compatible and work perfectly with any Sri Lankan ISP (Dialog, SLT, Mobitel, etc.) without any restrictions.

What is your refund policy? +

We offer a **100% money-back guarantee** if we are unable to deliver your order or resolve a technical issue within 48 hours. Your payment is safe with us.

Is my personal data safe? +

Absolutely. We only require the minimum necessary info (like your email for upgrades). We **never share your data** with third parties and use enterprise-grade encryption for all transactions.

How do I track my subscription expiry? +

You can see your plan details in your **Dashboard.** We also send a notification via WhatsApp 3 days before your subscription expires so you can renew it without any interruption.

Do I need to share my account password? +

No, we **never ask for your password.** For upgrades, you just need to accept an invitation or we provide a separate profile. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.

What if the service stops working before the expiry? +

This is extremely rare, but if it happens, you are covered by our **Full Replacement Warranty.** Simply contact our WhatsApp support, and we will restore your access or provide a new account within a few hours.

Are these services compatible with iOS/Apple devices? +

Currently, our packages are optimized and guaranteed to work on **Android Devices.** While some services might work on iOS, we cannot guarantee full compatibility or provide support for Apple devices at this time.

Can I change my plan later? +

Yes, certainly! You can **upgrade your plan** (e.g., from 3 Months to 1 Year) at any time. Just contact us, and we will adjust your remaining balance toward the new package.
