Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Since we sell digital goods, our policy is as follows:

Refund Eligibility

Refunds are only issued if:

  • The digital key is invalid and cannot be replaced.
  • The subscription fails to activate due to a technical error on our end.
  • Your order was charged but the key was never delivered (exceeding 24 hours).

Timeline

Refund requests must be made within 7 days of purchase. Once approved, the refund will be processed back to your original payment method within 3-5 business days.
